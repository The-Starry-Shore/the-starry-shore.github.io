    📖 Prism Spellcasting Rules▼

    All magic uses mana points instead of spell slots. These function somewhat the same except for the following:

    • Casters use a specific color of magic which changes the hue of their spells to an effect specific to that color.
    • You gain 1 mana point per round in combat (or 1/hr outside combat). Pre-combat mana is reset to 1 at start.
    • You can cast immediately or save mana points to cast higher level spells.
    • Some spells have an upkeep cost paid at the start of your turn.

    Spell Ascension

    • Upcasting a spell costs +1 Mana and +1 APC per level (max 7).
    • Upkeep spells have their cost increased by 1 Mana for each additional level.

    Progression Tree

    To advance to 2nd level spells, you must have at least 2 spells in each of that sphere's parent spheres.
