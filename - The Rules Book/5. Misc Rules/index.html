    Pets¶

    Characters can own “mundane pets”. These are not Animal Companions or Familiars. Acceptable "mundane pets" are in two categories: * Pets can be a maximum size of Medium and maximum Challenge Rating of ¼ all the way down to 0 CR. * Pets may be of Beast, Fey, Fiend, Construct or Dragon type. * Pets must have an intelligence of 3 or lower. * A character is limited to one maximum CR ¼ creature or two maximum CR ⅛ creatures.

    Each character may have up to two pets of a total CR ¼ or less, unless the character has purchased a pet via boosting or subscribing to the server:

    Boosting the server would let you have the following: * CR can be up to 1/2 * Size can be Large or smaller * Your pet can now be a Plant, Aberration, Ooze, Elemental, Monstrosity, beast, fey, fiend, construct or dragon.

    making the options up to 215 pets to choose from!! The size increase allows some pets to serve as mounts!

    Subscribing to the Server would let you have the following: * CR can be up to 3 for this pet * Intelligence can be up to 5 or lower * Size can be Large or smaller * Your pet can now be any type except giants

    making the options up to 384 pets to choose from!!

    What happens when a pet dies? are they gone forever? Nah, they spawn back in the Shore to be picked up for the next session. But they are out for the current mission unless they're revived.

    Downtime¶

    When the starry shorians are just chillin in The Starry Shore, they can spend their free time in a variety of activities such as: * Copying spells * Learning new tools or languages * Working for gold * Religious service to awaken/exalt an item * Changing spells choices * Crafting * Learning dunamancy or contaminations spells * Worshiping a god for their blessings * Change your subclass * Renting a 3rd artifact

    For more details check out the The Starry Shore DownTime Points Guide (DTP).

    Karma Points¶

    How to gain karma points¶

    For Players * When someone you invite to join the server reaches level 4 ==> 16 pts * Sharing a DND Beyond campaign with all the source books (not all the adventures lol) required to make a character ==> 12 pts * (you can get another 12 points when the first one is full, so let's all agree that sharing is caring)

    For DMs: * Becoming a full DM (one time thing) ==> 6 pts * Having a new player @Starling (New Player) (anyone who hasn't reached level 5 on any characters yet) in your session ==> 2 pts for each player * Running an open session when there's a lot of lfg ==> 2 pts (rewarded by the staff)

    For Counselors & Staff: * Helping a player correcting a creation log ==> 1 pts * Approving a creation log ==> 1 pts * Approving a respec log ==> 1 pts for each 3 lvls (Staff only) * Helping a new player through creating a character and typing a log ==> 4 pts

    Monthly Gain For Server Boosters & Subscribers: (once each month) * Noble Server Subscribers ==> 40 pts * Heir Server Subscribers ==> 20 pts * Steward Server Subscribers ==> 10 pts * Server Boosters ==> 10 pts (doesn't stack with multiple boosts)

    How to spend karma points¶

    • 4 pts ==> 1 hour on any character
    • 1 pts ==> 4 DTP
    • 1 pts ==> GP (value depends on character you redeem them on)
      • 5k for T1 & T2
      • 25k for T3
      • 75k for T4
      • 150k for T5
    • you can trade your karma points to prestige points!!
      • 1 Karma point = 2 Prestige pts
    • The Starry Shore Prestige Shopping List
      • You can pick from any of the taps except Others, Origins, and Racial Features!. (unless the character is already prestiged!)
      • P.S. You don't need to prestige to buy prestige stuff using the points gained from this
