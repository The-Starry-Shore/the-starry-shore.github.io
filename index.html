    D&D 5th Edition Westmarches Servers (2014 & 2024)¶


    Welcome to The Starry Shore Discover an endless number of worlds to explore at The Starry Shore! A place of respite adrift in the Astral Sea, the Starry Shore's magical barriers keep the typical dangers at bay, providing a sanctuary for any and all travelers who seek it. Adventure through new and exciting worlds via mysterious portals, or kick back with some friends and enjoy the Shore's amenities. We welcome travelers of all shapes and sizes and from all walks of life, so come let your imagination soar at The Starry Shore!
