    D&D 5th Edition Westmarches Servers (2014 & 2024)¶

    Welcome to The Starry Shore

    Join The Starry Shore — a vibrant 18+ Dungeons & Dragons 5E and 5.5E community! New players Super Friendly. Explore the multiverse as creative and talented DMs guide you through one-shots, campaigns, and living worlds of their own imagining. All official magic items are available.

    Participate in live voice and text role-play, and experience the thrill of adventure anytime, anywhere. With an active and friendly player base, it's time to let your imagination soar at The Starry Shore! Join our community of players and start your journey today!

    • Super easy character creation & starting at lvl 3
    • Beginner and veteran players & DMs friendly, for both D&D 5e and Avrae
    • LGBT+ Friendly Space
    • Special Gods with blessings for worshiping them
    • Beyond campaigns you can join for making a character with all the choices
    • 24/7 Available games for all timezones
    • Hours based character leveling
    • DM Rewards gets you 1.5 times character progression

    Join The Starry Shore today and experience limitless storytelling across two timelines — 2014 (5e) and 2024 (5.5e).
