    The Starry Shore¶

    The Starry Shore: A D&D 5th Edition Westmarches Server¶

    Welcome to The Starry Shore¶

    Discover an endless number of worlds to explore at The Starry Shore! A place of respite adrift in the Astral Sea, the Starry Shore's magical barriers keep the typical dangers at bay, providing a sanctuary for any and all travelers who seek it. Adventure through new and exciting worlds via mysterious portals, or kick back with some friends and enjoy the Shore's amenities. We welcome travelers of all shapes and sizes and from all walks of life, so come let your imagination soar at The Starry Shore!

    How Things Work here?¶

    Player Characters (PCs) live in The Starry Shore, an interplanar hub with the main street leading to many portals. Dungeon Masters create those portals leading to whatever worlds they dream up. PCs can visit those worlds via live sessions and/or text role play (TRP). PCs earn levels and gold via hours played in live or text sessions, and can level up beyond 20 by multiclassing, with no cap. Magic items are plentiful and most are easy to get.

    Most of the games offered here are exciting one-shots, perfect for those who love spontaneous adventures. There are a few DMs that host campaigns, Typically, they like to play a few sessions with new players to see if there’s a good fit. If they like your gaming style, they might invite you to join a campaign. This server mainly focuses on these one-shot games, which make up the majority of our activities, allowing you to play on your own schedule.

    We’re proud to say that our community only includes the coolest players. That said, we embrace diversity in play styles. Whether you’re someone who revels in rich storytelling and immersive roleplay, or a strategist who enjoys optimizing characters to their fullest potential, you’ll find a welcoming space here. Everyone is invited to bring their unique approach to the game.

    Member Responsibilities¶

    • Have common sense, and follow the Server Code of Conduct.
    • You must be 18 years of age or older to play on the server (This is both for social and legal reasons. Sorry, no exceptions.).
    • PVP requires consent from both parties including the DM. Consent needs to be communicated, not just implied.
    • Sexual themes are not allowed until consent from the whole party is given.
    • Adult themes and language may appear. Our DM world's range from lighthearted and funny to dark and serious.
    • You need an approved character in order to play (see Getting Started).
    • Players must track their characters (level, gold, items, etc) and keep this information updated in the player folder provided, as well as the relevant log channels.
    • In a rules dispute between a DM and Player, the DM’s word goes until the session is over and the staff can mediate.
    • Unsure of something? Ask away! Our community is welcoming, knowledgeable, and happy to help.

    Open Door Policy¶

    The staff team’s goal is to promote a safe and fun environment for all server members. You are invited to contact any staff through voice or private messages with questions or concerns as you see fit. You can submit your thoughts any time through the #tickets channel, where the staff team will have access to it for review and response.

    Character Creation Steps (#getting-started)¶

    1. Make sure to watch our intro Play 24/7 online D&D games on The Starry Shore discord server!!
    2. Then fill in this form to make a folder for you, which will appear in the Google Drive of the email that you provide. You only need to fill out this form once. A Google account is required to share your player folder with you.
      https://forms.gle/Fk5GDGm1MxKUwTNC9
    3. (Make sure that you did step one :D) Create a level 3 character. Use 35 point buy, not stat rolls and fixed hp, not rolled. Use this calculator for your core stats:
      http://chicken-dinner.com/5e/5e-point-buy.html#select&NA&8&8&8&8&8&8&0&0&35&18&8&19&15&12&9&7&5&4&3&2&1&0&1&2&4&6&9&4&4&4&4&4&4
    4. For your sheet, you have 3 options:
    5. D&D Beyond: Which is the easiest if you're a beginner. If you choose this, please go to your folder in the Google Drive and put a link to your sheet in the red part in your tracking sheet.
      P.S. there's a thread for some tips on how to use it i #getting-started
    6. Dicecloud: Same as Beyond, please go to your folder in the google drive and put a link to your sheet in the red part in your tracking sheet.
    7. G-sheet: In your Google Drive folder, there is a blank Gsheet that you can copy and rename it to your character's name. (you don't need to use this if you're using Beyond or Dicecloud)
    8. Post your character in the Creation Log channel, using the pinned post for a template. Use this exact template Creation Log Template
    9. You can find a creation log cheat sheet to help you make your creation log, you can find your folder here How to find my folder
    10. And if you need help, feel free to open a ticket with the counselors to help you with the log #create-a-ticket
      P.S. For races with multiple versions from different sourcebooks, you must list the source you are choosing.
    11. Once your character is ✅ Staff Approved, you receive the Player role, you are ready to play!
      Click on the Roles channel and click on @Tier 1 (lvl 3-4) to receive pings about new games.
      P.S. Players can take either max gold from what their starting class gives them or starting equipment.

    How to find your folder:¶

    If you still can't find your folder make sure to check that:
    you did the first step one in the Character Creation Steps
     you're logged in the same google account you input in the form in step one

    Creation Tips¶

    • Use one of two base equipment options:
    • Starting equipment granted by class and background
    • Max values for the starting gold rolls of your 1st class
    • Average hp, not rolled. Take the maximum HP for first level and the average HP for all levels after
    • Multiclassing is allowed
    • Background customization is allowed
    • Can use any content listed in Allowed Materials
    • Additional content rules:
    • Artificers must track their Infusions

    Note that all new characters begin with a special Sending Stone, as specified below under the Magic Items section of the rules.

    Allowed Material (Reference For Most Material)¶

    Official Content:¶

    All the officially printed races/sub-races, classes/sub-classes, backgrounds, feats, and spells by Wizards of the Coast for Dungeons and Dragons 5th Edition.

    P.S. Player Hand Book 2024 / 5.5E is Not approved yet

    Partnered Content:¶

    • Critical Role (except Gunslinger subclass): Explorer's Guide to Wildemount, Critical Role: Call of the Netherdeep, Tal’Dorei Campaign Setting Reborn
    • P.S. Mystic Conflux can only be taken at T4/level 17 and above
    • Grim Hollow: Lairs of Etharis (except the feats), Grim Hollow: Player Pack (except Blade Breaker subclass)
    • The Griffon’s Saddlebag: Book Two (except Warlock: The Many)
    • Other: Humblewood Campaign Setting, Humblewood Tales, Dungeons of Drakkenheim, Flee, Mortals!, Where Evil Lives, In Search of the Smuggler's Secrets, Dungeons & Dragons vs. Rick and Morty, Frozen Sick, Monstrous Compendium Vol. 3: Minecraft Creatures, Lightning Keep, Tome of Beasts 1, Obojima: Tales from the Tall Grass
    • Steinhardt's Guide to The Eldritch Hunt (except Blade of Radiance and Living Nightmare subclass, backgrounds, items, cannoneer and optional replacement feats)
    • P.S. For Jaeger:
    • Multiclassing Prerequisites: Ability Score Minimum: Strength 13 or Dexterity 13
    • Multiclassing Proficiencies Gained: Light armor, medium armor, simple weapons, martial weapons, firearm

    Banned Content:¶

    • Kobold Press: Book of Ebon Tides, Tales from the Shadows
    • The Illrigger Revised, The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying, Grim Hollow: Player Guide

    Character Slots¶

    Players are allowed to have as many characters as they want, The only limitation is your creativity :D
    If you’re gonna be abusing it, remember, the gods of the Starry Shore are always watching!

    Live Sessions¶

    DMs post their upcoming sessions in #looking-to-host. If you don't see a live session there that fits your schedule, you can ask if DMs are interested in hosting in the #looking-for-game channel. When you post in LFG, you can say something about your character(s), what kind of game you’re looking for and what times you can play.
    Follow the directions in the LTH post carefully. Each DM may require different information to join. Send the DM a private message to ask questions or with the information requested in their post.
    Enjoy the session!
    After the session, the DM will post a #session-log. You will need to update your character’s tracking sheet with your hours, items, and gold gained or lost in the session.

    If you have a disagreement with a DM, we encourage reaching out to them outside of the game to work things out. If there are still difficulties, or if this is not possible, please contact a staff member to help resolve the issue.
