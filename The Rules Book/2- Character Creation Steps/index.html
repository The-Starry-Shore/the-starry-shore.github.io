    2 Character Creation Steps

    Character Creation Steps (#getting-started)¶

    1. Make sure to watch our intro Play 24/7 online D&D games on The Starry Shore discord server!!
    2. Then fill in this form to make a folder for you, which will appear in the Google Drive of the email that you provide. You only need to fill out this form once. A Google account is required to share your player folder with you. https://forms.gle/Fk5GDGm1MxKUwTNC9
    3. (Make sure that you did step one :D) Create a level 3 character. Use 35 point buy, not stat rolls and fixed HP (do not roll for hit points). Use this calculator for your core stats: http://chicken-dinner.com/5e/5e-point-buy.html#select&NA&8&8&8&8&8&8&0&0&35&18&8&19&15&12&9&7&5&4&3&2&1&0&1&2&4&6&9&4&4&4&4&4&4
    4. For your sheet, you have 3 options:
      • D&D Beyond: Which is the easiest if you're a beginner. If you choose this, please go to your folder in the Google Drive and put a link to your sheet in the red part in your tracking sheet. P.S. there's a thread for some tips on how to use it in #getting-started
      • Dicecloud: Same as Beyond, please go to your folder in the google drive and put a link to your sheet in the red part in your tracking sheet.
      • G-sheet: In your Google Drive folder, there is a blank Gsheet that you can copy and rename it to your character's name. (you don't need to use this if you're using Beyond or Dicecloud)
    5. Post your character in the Creation Log channel, using the pinned post for a template. Use this exact template Creation Log Template
    6. A creation log cheat sheet is available in your folder to help you. For instructions on locating your folder, see the section titled How to find my folder.
    7. And if you need help, feel free to open a ticket with the counselors to help you with the log #create-a-ticket P.S. For races with multiple versions from different sourcebooks, you must list the source you are choosing.
    8. Once your character is ✅ Staff Approved, you receive the Player role, you are ready to play! Click on the Roles channel and click on @Tier 1 (lvl 3-4) to receive pings about new games. P.S. Players can take either max gold from what their starting class gives them or starting equipment.

    How to find your folder:¶

    If you still can't find your folder make sure to check that: * you completed step one in the Character Creation Steps * you're logged in the same google account you input in the form in step one

    Creation Tips¶

    • Use one of two base equipment options:
      • Starting equipment granted by class and background
      • Max values for the starting gold rolls of your 1st class
    • Average hp, not rolled. Take the maximum HP for first level and the average HP for all levels after
    • Multiclassing is allowed
    • Background customization is allowed
    • Can use any content listed in Allowed Materials
    • Additional content rules:
      • Artificers must track their Infusions

    Note that all new characters begin with a special Sending Stone, as specified below under the Magic Items section of the rules.

    Allowed Material¶

    Official Content:¶

    All the officially printed races/sub-races, classes/sub-classes, backgrounds, feats, and spells by Wizards of the Coast for Dungeons and Dragons 5th Edition.

    P.S. The 2024 Player's Handbook (One D&D/5.5E) is not approved for use in this server, instead check out our 2024 server.

    Partnered Content:¶

    • Critical Role (except Gunslinger subclass): Explorer's Guide to Wildemount, Critical Role: Call of the Netherdeep, Tal’Dorei Campaign Setting Reborn
      • P.S. Mystic Conflux can only be taken at T4/level 17 and above
    • Grim Hollow: Lairs of Etharis (except the feats), Grim Hollow: Player Pack (except Blade Breaker subclass)
    • The Griffon’s Saddlebag: Book Two (except Warlock: The Many)
    • Other: Humblewood Campaign Setting, Humblewood Tales, Dungeons of Drakkenheim, Flee, Mortals!, Where Evil Lives, In Search of the Smuggler's Secrets, Dungeons & Dragons vs. Rick and Morty, Frozen Sick, Monstrous Compendium Vol. 3: Minecraft Creatures, Lightning Keep, Tome of Beasts 1, Obojima: Tales from the Tall Grass
    • Steinhardt's Guide to The Eldritch Hunt (except Blade of Radiance and Living Nightmare subclass, backgrounds, items, cannoneer and optional replacement feats)
      • P.S. For Jaeger:
        • Multiclassing Prerequisites: Ability Score Minimum: Strength 13 or Dexterity 13
        • Multiclassing Proficiencies Gained: Light armor, medium armor, simple weapons, martial weapons, firearm

    Banned Content:¶

    • Kobold Press: Book of Ebon Tides, Tales from the Shadows
    • The Illrigger Revised, The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying, Grim Hollow: Player Guide

    Character Slots¶

    Players are allowed to have as many characters as they want, The only limitation is your creativity :D If you’re gonna be abusing it, remember, the gods of the Starry Shore are always watching!

    Live Sessions¶

    DMs post their upcoming sessions in #looking-to-host. If you don't see a live session there that fits your schedule, you can ask if DMs are interested in hosting in the #looking-for-game channel. When you post in LFG, you can say something about your character(s), what kind of game you’re looking for and what times you can play. Follow the directions in the LTH post carefully. Each DM may require different information to join. Send the DM a private message to ask questions or with the information requested in their post. Enjoy the session! After the session, the DM will post a #session-log. You will need to update your character’s tracking sheet with your hours, items, and gold gained or lost in the session.

    If you have a disagreement with a DM, we encourage reaching out to them outside of the game to work things out. If there are still difficulties, or if this is not possible, please contact a staff member to help resolve the issue.
